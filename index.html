What I do

Three things I bring to every learning problem

Good instructional design isn’t one skill. It’s knowing how people learn, knowing how to hold their attention, and being willing to understand a hard topic deeply before trying to teach it.

01

Teaching instinct

As a teaching assistant at Texas A&M (2021–2025), I taught 2,000+ first-year engineering students — 400+ every semester. I learned where explanations break and how to sequence material so a beginner climbs from lost to capable.

02

Designer’s craft

From building apps, games, and VR, I treat a lesson like a level: clear goals, a fair difficulty curve, fast feedback, and no wasted friction. I work in Captivate, Figma, and Adobe CC.

03

Researcher’s patience

With a background spanning engineering and visualization research, I’ll sit with a subject-matter expert and dig until the system makes sense, then write objectives backward from there.
